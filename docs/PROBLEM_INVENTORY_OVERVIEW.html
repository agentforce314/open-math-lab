1. How to read difficulty

  • Math level — how hard the mathematics is: U undergraduate / olympiad · G graduate / named theorem · R genuinely open research.
  • Lab-status tier — what is actually machine-checked in proofs/lean-project/ProofLab/:

2. The genuinely open problems (T4 — research level)

hou-zeng-pfc open · score 84

Hou–Zeng conjecture: every integer n > 4 is a sum odd prime + positive Fibonacci + Catalan number. (Sun 2009, OEIS A154404.)

Prize: $1,000 proof / $200 counterexample (Hou / Zeng) — do not claim.

What the lab holds: ProofLab/HouZeng.lean zero-sorry: predicate IsOddPrimeFibCatalan, hou_zeng_five, bounded hou_zeng_le_N for 5 ≤ n ≤ 30 via native_decide. Already verified to 5·10¹³ by McNeil, so a counterexample is not a one-wave target.

krenn-gu open · score 80

Krenn–Gu conjecture (quantum-optics graphs): for even N ≥ 6 and D ≥ 3 colours, no edge-weighted complete graph on N vertices has a "perfectly monochromatic" perfect-matching weight system in D colours (each vertex sees each colour with total weight from a fixed equation system). Equivalently: certain multi-photon GHZ states cannot be produced by linear optics without ancillas.

Prize: €3,000 (Krenn / Leitner) — do not claim.

What the lab holds: ProofLab/KrennGu.lean zero-sorry: EqSystem / pmSum encoding over ℤ, positive C₄ witness (D=2). Attack OPE-1038: exhaustive (8,3) and (6,3) searches over ring {−1,0,1} → 0 counterexample witnesses. Namesake ∀N,D not attempted.

Rejected as primes (OPE-1028): Millennium $1M (no sub-bounty lemma), Beal $1M (FLT-class), Erdős #78 constructive Ramsey (not finitely resolvable), RSA challenge (ended 2007), sun-135 (proved 2020, archived).
